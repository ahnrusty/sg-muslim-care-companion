# What to ask the doctors in ICU

Use this checklist in family meetings. You do not need to ask everything at once. Pick what is most useful right now.

## Prognosis and goals

1. Is the condition terminal and irreversible? In Islamic jurisprudence, *la yurja bur'uh* (no realistic hope of recovery) is the threshold for considering withdrawal or withholding of life-sustaining treatment [^1][^4].
2. Is the current treatment aimed at cure, or at keeping organs functioning artificially?
3. What is the realistic prognosis with and without each intervention? Numbers help.
4. What does "improvement" look like, in your experience, in cases like this?

## Documentation already on file

5. Has the patient registered an Advance Medical Directive (AMD)? AMD status is confidential until terminal illness is certified. Ask the clinical team to do an AMD search.
6. Is there an Advance Care Planning document (ACP) on file? ACP guides decisions even though it is not legally binding [^1][^2].
7. Does the patient have a Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA), and if so, who is the donee for personal welfare?
8. What is the patient's HOTA opt-out status? Relevant if brain death is being considered.

## Decision-making process

9. How much time do we have before a decision is required? MUIS guidance says family should consult and reach agreement [^1].
10. Who is the single point of contact in the medical team? What is the escalation path?
11. Can a palliative care or hospital ethics consultation be arranged? Most public hospitals in Singapore have palliative care teams [^3].
12. If a DNR is in place or being considered, what other care continues? The expected answer is "everything else." See `dnr-and-withdrawal.md`.

## Spiritual and practical needs

13. Can a Muslim religious officer (asatizah) or chaplain visit at the bedside? Wards will facilitate this. Families can also contact a local mosque directly. MUIS hotline **6359 1199** [^3].
14. Can we have quiet time and privacy for Quran recitation and prayer at the bedside?
15. Can the team avoid scheduling non-urgent procedures during the five daily prayer times where possible?
16. If the patient is in the final hours, how will the team support *talqin* and family being at the bedside? See `20-imminent-death/talqin.md`.

## After death

17. What is the body-release process after death? Muslim families typically need release within hours to begin *ghusl*, *kafan*, and burial within 24 hours.
18. Who completes the Certificate of Cause of Death (CCOD), and how will the digital death certificate reach us? See `40-singapore-admin/ccod-and-ica.md`.
19. If brain death is declared and HOTA applies, what is the timeline for organ donation and body release? See `organ-donation.md`.

## When in doubt

If something is unclear, ask again in plain language. You are allowed to say "I do not understand, please explain it differently."

For religious questions, MUIS hotline **6359 1199**, or your local mosque imam. This page is not a substitute for a doctor or for an asatizah.
