## Bereavement-relevant services

Many MUIS mosques provide some combination of the services below. The list reflects what is commonly available; phone the mosque to confirm what they currently offer.

- ARS-certified asatizah typically available for *talqin*, *solat jenazah*, and family du'a.
- May offer *kursus pengurusan jenazah* (Muslim funeral management training) on a periodic basis.
- Social Development Officer for MUIS zakat assistance enquiries (at Social Development Mosques).
- *Solat jenazah* coordination at the mosque before burial.

## What to ask when you call

- Is an *imam* available today for *solat jenazah* at the mosque?
- Can the mosque send an *ustaz* for bedside *talqin* at our address or hospital?
- Are there community *pengurus jenazah* contacts the mosque can refer us to?
- For Muslim zakat or social assistance: who is the Social Development Officer and what are their hours?

If the mosque cannot help directly, ask for a referral. The community network is wide.
