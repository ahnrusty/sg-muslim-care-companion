## A sample answer (calm, plain English, cited)

Take a breath. You do not need to do everything in the next ten minutes.

At the bedside:

- Sit. Say *Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un*.
- Gently close the eyes and mouth. Cover the body with a clean sheet.
- Recite the *shahadah*, *al-Fatihah*, and *al-Ikhlas* softly. Quiet tears are fine; loud wailing is discouraged in Islam.

In hospital:

- Tell the nurse. The doctor will certify death and issue the Certificate of Cause of Death online. Since May 2022, this auto-registers the death with ICA, and you will download the digital death certificate from My Legacy with your Singpass.
- Ask how long the body can stay before being moved, so the family can begin arrangements.

Calls to make:

- One Muslim funeral service provider. Choose one, not many. They will project-manage the next 24 to 48 hours.
- Family. Short messages are best.
- MUIS at **6359 1199** for coordination, especially if the deceased has no close family.
