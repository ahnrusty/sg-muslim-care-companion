## A sample answer

Muslim burial booking in Singapore goes through NEA, not MUIS.

Two steps your funeral director typically handles:

1. **Permit to Bury**, free, via the NEA ePortal. Requires the digital death certificate number, the deceased's details, and the next-of-kin's NRIC.
2. **Burial slot**, booked at the **Choa Chu Kang Cemetery Complex**, 940 Old Choa Chu Kang Road. Tel **6225 5632**, Mon to Sun 8:30am to 4:30pm (closed 1pm to 2pm).

Fee: **$315** for a Singapore citizen or PR adult plot, $140 for a child below 10, on a 15-year lease.

Bring the digital death certificate, the deceased's NRIC, the Permit to Bury, your NRIC, and a letter of authorisation if you are not a direct next-of-kin.

For religious coordination, MUIS hotline **6359 1199**. **Masjid Pusara Aman** is at **6792 9378**.
