## A sample answer

A DNR (Do Not Resuscitate) order tells the medical team not to attempt CPR if the heart stops. It does not stop other care. Comfort, dignity, and pain relief continue.

There is no standalone MUIS fatwa titled "DNR", but the framework that applies is the same one MUIS set out for the Advance Medical Directive in 1994: Muslims may refuse extraordinary life-sustaining treatment when terminally ill and unable to decide. The Federation of Islamic Medical Associations also confirms DNR consent is permissible when the prognosis is futile.

What to ask the team before deciding:

- Is the condition terminal and irreversible?
- If CPR were attempted, what is the realistic chance and quality of survival?
- Will pain control and other care continue if a DNR is in place? Yes is the expected answer.

This is not a fatwa. For your specific situation, please consult your asatizah or call MUIS at **6359 1199**, and discuss with the treating doctor.
