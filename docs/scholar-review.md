# Scholar review

This document tracks how the corpus has been or should be reviewed by qualified people before users rely on it.

## Why this matters

The corpus touches fiqh, medicine, and law. Errors can mislead a grieving family. The companion's value depends on its accuracy.

## Recommended review panel

- At least one **asatizah recognised under the Asatizah Recognition Scheme** for the fiqh content in `corpus/10-critical-illness/`, `corpus/20-imminent-death/`, `corpus/30-fardhu-kifayah/`, `corpus/50-after-burial/`, and `corpus/60-inheritance/`.
- At least one **palliative care doctor or medical social worker** for `corpus/10-critical-illness/` and the medical framing in `corpus/20-imminent-death/`.
- At least one **pengurus jenazah** with active experience in Singapore for `corpus/30-fardhu-kifayah/` and `corpus/40-singapore-admin/funeral-service-providers.md`.
- At least one **Syariah-experienced lawyer** for `corpus/60-inheritance/`.
- At least one **community worker** from MUIS, AMP, PERGAS, or MENDAKI for `corpus/70-financial-and-community/`.

## How to seek review

1. Open a GitHub issue titled "Review request: <topic>" and tag the relevant areas of the corpus.
2. Or, if you are an asatizah, doctor, or lawyer reading this, open a pull request with corrections and indicate your qualification in the PR description.
3. The maintainer will record acknowledgement in the table below after review is completed.

## Open questions for review

- Brain death determination: mainstream Singapore-MUIS position vs other respected scholarly views. Is the corpus framing balanced?
- Withdrawal vs withholding of life-sustaining treatment: is the framing aligned with the latest MUIS Office of the Mufti position?
- Photographs at janazah: corpus presents discouragement with limited exceptions. Is this in line with current local guidance?
- Length of tahlil gatherings (3, 7, 40, 100 days): corpus notes scholarly disagreement on whether these are accepted local custom or bid'ah. Asatizah review wanted on the balance.
- Women attending burial in Singapore: corpus presents discouragement with note on permissibility. Asatizah review wanted.
- CPF nomination interaction with faraid: corpus reflects the CPF Act override. Lawyer review wanted on edge cases.
