## Notes on accuracy

Public guidance, fees, online portals, cemetery policies, and forms change. The corpus is a best effort to summarise authoritative sources at the time of writing. Always confirm current information with the relevant agency, mosque, or scholar before acting. See `scholar-review.md` for the review plan and `DISCLAIMER.md` for the project's full disclaimer.
