# Disclaimer

SG Muslim Care Companion is a community-benefit navigator. It exists to help Muslim families in Singapore find the right people and ask the right questions during critical illness, end-of-life, and bereavement.

## It is not a substitute for

1. A qualified asatizah recognised under the Asatizah Recognition Scheme (ARS) in Singapore. Religious rulings should be confirmed with the Office of the Mufti or your local mosque imam.
2. A licensed medical professional. Medical decisions, including DNR, withdrawal of treatment, palliative care choices, and brain death determination, must be made with the treating doctor and the medical team caring for the patient.
3. A qualified Syariah lawyer. Legal and inheritance decisions should involve the Syariah Court of Singapore or a Muslim lawyer with relevant Syariah experience.

## Accuracy

The corpus is a best effort to summarise authoritative public sources at the time of writing. Public guidance, fees, online portals, cemetery policies, and forms change. Always confirm current information with the relevant agency or scholar before acting.

## Scholarly disagreement

On topics where qualified scholars hold different positions, the companion presents the mainstream Singapore-MUIS position first, then briefly notes other respected views, and recommends asatizah consultation. The companion does not issue fatwa.

## Privacy

The default chat path runs locally on your machine using Ollama. No personal data is sent to a remote service unless you opt in by configuring `OPENAI_BASE_URL`. There is no telemetry.

If you choose to use a cloud large language model, your questions and the retrieved corpus context will be sent to that provider according to their terms.

## How to seek help

If you are in distress, call:

- Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) 24-hour hotline: 1767
- IMH Mental Health Helpline: 6389 2222
- Police: 999, Ambulance: 995

For asatizah guidance, contact your local mosque or the Office of the Mufti via [muis.gov.sg/officeofthemufti](https://www.muis.gov.sg/officeofthemufti).
